What it checks

Built for a store that already exports its time clock to Excel. Your rules are set once and used every week.

Break rules

No more than 6 hours in a row without a break, plus coffee and dinner breaks. Every number is adjustable.

Schedule vs. actual

Red marks for coming in early, leaving late or working more hours than scheduled, with the minutes shown.

Overtime

Hours over 40 a week (and optionally over 12 in a day), with an estimated cost using each person's pay rate.

Print and export

Printable week grids, edited-punch reports, schedule cards with store rules, and Excel downloads.

How it works

Three steps each week.

  1. Export the weekSave the time-clock export (Excel) as you already do.
  2. Upload itSign in and choose the file. Several weeks at once is fine.
  3. Review and printOpen the flagged people, fix punches with a note, then print or download.

Private by design

  • Accounts are created by the store owner and sent by invitation. Nobody can sign up on their own.
  • Schedules, reports and employee files are encrypted on your own device before they are saved. The server only stores scrambled data it cannot read.
  • Nothing is shared with advertisers or other companies.
  • The screen locks itself when nobody is using it.
About signing in The sign-in page asks only for your store number, the username your manager gave you, and your Punch Verification password. We will never ask for a Google, email, bank or card password, and we never ask you to download anything.
