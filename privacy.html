Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This website and its connected automation tools are operated for internal business use by All Fresh Supermarket. This page explains how information is handled.

Google account access

Our internal automation system connects to our own Google account to create and update files in our Google Drive and Google Sheets and to send email notifications. This access is used only by our own business and only for these purposes. We do not sell, rent, or share Google user data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Information we process

When customers contact us by phone, we may record the call and store details they provide (such as name, phone number, address, and program information) so that we can process their request. This information is stored in our own Google Drive and Google Sheets and is used only to provide our services.

Automated calls

Customers who have given consent may receive automated informational calls about their account. Anyone may opt out at any time by pressing 9 during the call or by contacting our customer service desk at 845-578-1700.

Data retention and deletion

We keep information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected. To ask us to review or delete your information, contact us using the details below.
